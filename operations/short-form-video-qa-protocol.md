# BruA1 Empire — Short-Form Video Production and QA Protocol

Approved by owner: 2026-11-20.

## Objective
Improve audience retention and clarity for BruA1 music-production Shorts without sacrificing instructional accuracy or brand identity.

## Production standards
1. In the first 1–2 seconds, demonstrate the audio/visual result or show the concrete problem and promised fix. Avoid generic intros.
2. For a single quick FL Studio tip, aim for 25–30 seconds; use longer durations only when the demonstration requires them.
3. Put the formula, key action, or main takeaway early. Do not delay the core answer until the middle of the video.
4. Remove dead air, repeated steps, slow plugin navigation, and unnecessary outro content.
5. Make the result clear both sonically and visually. Use accurate captions and readable on-screen labels; preserve relevant plugin/settings context.
6. End promptly after delivering the useful takeaway, with a concise call to action only if it does not weaken retention.
7. Avoid misleading hooks or oversimplification. Prioritize correctness and original BruA1 content.

## Pre-publication QA
- Opening demonstrates value within 2 seconds.
- Audio result is audible, appropriately leveled, and not clipped.
- Text/captions are legible on a mobile screen and technically accurate.
- Timing and pacing suit the lesson; any duration above 30 seconds is justified.
- Video title, description, branding, and publishing destination are correct.
- No unlicensed third-party content is introduced.
- Flag unclear or technically incorrect content for revision; do not claim QA clearance without reviewing the actual export.

Snapshot is preliminary and not a permanent benchmark.
- After publishing, review stayed-to-watch rate, average view duration, percentage viewed, engagement, and relative performance when data becomes available.
- Compare like-for-like Shorts and test one meaningful change at a time (opening, length, demo timing, or on-screen text).
- Analytics & Growth Manager should relay specific findings to Video Production Manager and QA. Do not assert that an external bot received the update until confirmed.
- Keep existing posting cadence unless separately approved.
