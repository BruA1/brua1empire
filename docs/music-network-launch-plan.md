# BruA1 Music Network — founding release
Owner approved the community concept: affordable membership, professional profiles, searchable directory, internal forum, channel chat, private messages. Proposed $4.99/month and $49.99/year; subscription terms and billing not yet published.
## Separation
Public preview at /music-network.html. Separate network database and separate API Worker planned. No changes to instrumental checkout, orders, PayPal merchant secrets or R2 masters. Public asset allowlist includes only the three network presentation files.
## Sequence
1. Public preview with clearly labeled example profiles and discussions; no payment, data collection or sign-up.
2. Account authentication, email verification, secure session lifecycle, rate limits and separate D1 migration. Member status, paid entitlement and expiry enforced server-side on every private API operation, never based on browser flags.
3. Member-consented profile visibility, multiple professional roles and searchable directory. Public visitor sees only opt-in fields; no email or private data.
4. Member-only persistent forum threads, replies and chat; safe text rendering, reporting and blocking. Admin authorization, moderation audit, abuse handling and privacy controls are required before opening beta.
5. Private messaging with authorization of both parties, blocking, rate limits, reports; never expose other people's messages.
6. PayPal sandbox recurring subscriptions; webhook signature, entitlement updates, renewal/cancellation/expiry, nonpayment handling and audit. Separate tax classification from instrumental licenses; transparent recurring billing and cancellation terms. Live billing remains off pending verification.
7. Founding beta with real approved members, then public launch. No fabricated users or member counts.
## Data
Schema: workers/brua1-music-network/schema.sql. Local isolated prototype test only. No production community database, credentials, registrations or subscriptions activated by this commit.

## Invitation-only beta foundation
Single-use 256-bit invitation, one-time issued 256-bit login key, 12-hour hashed sessions, logout/revocation and separate active membership enforcement. These are private beta credentials, not a public email/password registration service; a secure invitation distribution/recovery process and email-based identity verification or managed sign-in are still required for wider launch. No account creation or billing enabled by deployment. Separate empty D1 and locked Worker provisioning workflow performs live readback without touching commerce.

## Expanded professional directory and community (November 25)
The same membership is intended to include musicians, producers, artists, carpenters, contractors and other service professionals, not just music. Members can choose up to ten roles, describe skills/services and city/service area, indicate availability, and opt into the member-only directory. Keep the existing music role table; add a separate constrained service-role table without destructive migration. The member workspace includes an editable profile, search and profession filtering, persistent forum and a shared General chat that refreshes every five seconds while visible; this is near-real-time polling, not WebSocket presence. No fake member counts, no public exposure of member details, no transaction/booking marketplace or verification badge implied. Before launch: confirm real PayPal sandbox approval, public registration and recovery, moderation/report UI, renewal handling, service-specific tax review and user acceptance of terms; preserve all live billing locks.

## Member-only instrumental discounts (CEO requirement, November 25)
Both active monthly and annual subscribers should receive automatic member pricing on eligible BruA1 instrumental licenses. Discount percentage, eligible tiers, stacking rules and any exclusions require CEO confirmation before implementation or advertisement as an available offer. Keep the existing public catalog and license rights unchanged. At checkout, authenticate the member and verify paid entitlement server-side against the membership service using a narrowly scoped, authenticated service binding; never accept a member flag, percentage, coupon or adjusted price supplied by the browser. Fail safely to clearly disclosed regular pricing if entitlement cannot be verified, allowing the customer to review before payment. Calculate and record original price, discount, final subtotal, applicable tax, currency and entitlement-check timestamp in the server-side order quote; confirm the exact PayPal amount before capture, preserve fulfillment and license checks, and do not discount subscriptions themselves. Test active monthly and annual, canceled-but-still-paid, expired, unpaid, suspended, duplicate requests, tampered amounts, quote expiry, tax, refunds and checkout service failure. No real payments, billing flags, public prices or rates changed by this requirement.
