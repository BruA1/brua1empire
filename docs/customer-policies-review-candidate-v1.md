# BruA1 customer policies — review candidate 1
Prepared 2026-11-23. Internal draft; not a published policy or legal approval. Seller identity and required contact/address disclosures must be completed from restricted records. Do not enable checkout until actual implementation and review match these promises.

## Proposed refund and cancellation policy
BruA1 sells digital instrumental licenses, not ownership of the underlying instrumental. Before payment, the buyer receives the applicable license, publishing schedule, price and any applicable tax. No payment is required for previews or inquiries.
- A buyer may cancel an unpaid order at any time. For an accidental or duplicate paid purchase, contact brua1@brua1.com promptly with the order number. Proposed voluntary window: seven calendar days for an unused license with no audio downloaded or exploitation of the music; subject to verification and mandatory rights.
- For a missing, corrupt, incorrect or materially nonconforming delivery, contact support. BruA1 will investigate and offer correction, replacement delivery or an appropriate refund when correction is not reasonably possible. Mandatory consumer rights, PayPal and card issuer dispute rights are not restricted by this policy.
- Change-of-mind refunds after a successful download or exercise of the license are not ordinarily offered, except where law or an applicable payment-provider rule requires otherwise. No blanket waiver of statutory rights.
- Approved refunds return through the original payment method where available. Unused download authorizations are revoked; the final agreement must specify the consequences for existing recordings and previously exercised rights. Partial refunds, reversals and disputes require review; do not represent a dispute as a final termination.
- If checkout is unavailable, contact support; an inquiry is not a license or order.

## Proposed privacy notice
BruA1 uses information necessary to operate the store and fulfill instrumental licenses: buyer name and email, product/tier, acceptance and publishing schedule records, payment identifiers/status, receipts, download and support records, and limited security logs. PayPal processes payment information under its own privacy notice; BruA1 must not collect or store full card numbers or PayPal passwords. Cloudflare hosts the site and proposed order and private-file services. Any receipt-email provider must be identified before publication.
Information is used for fulfillment, license records, customer support, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution and applicable legal obligations. Do not use purchase emails for marketing without an appropriate separate basis and unsubscribe mechanism. Proposed practice: no sale of customer personal information, no unnecessary tracking, restrict staff access, use expiring download authorizations, and avoid logging secrets. Verify these practices before publication.
Retention: establish and disclose actual retention by category (orders/licenses and tax records, support, security logs and expiring tokens); retain only what is needed or legally required, with documented deletion and exceptions. Requests for access, correction or deletion may be sent to brua1@brua1.com; verify identity proportionately and honor applicable rights. Confirm jurisdiction-specific rights, cross-border processing, contact/address disclosures, cookie/analytics inventory, minors policy and any required opt-outs before release.

## Internal approval and implementation checklist
- Confirm legal seller disclosures, support inbox, approved refund window, refund/termination treatment, applicable privacy and consumer rights, processor list and retention.
- Verify taxes with qualified adviser: Louisiana generally taxes digital audio works; a commercial IP-interest exclusion may apply depending on the transaction. Do not assume every instrumental license is taxable or exempt. Confirm sourcing, registration, nexus and rate handling.
- Record acceptance of exact agreement and track schedule version/hash, buyer-specific delivery evidence and verified PayPal capture. Preserve receipts and provider evidence without storing credentials.
- Finalize dated immutable public policies and record their hashes before release. No flags are authorized by this draft.
