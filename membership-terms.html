Membership and recurring payments

Proposed plans: $4.99 USD each month or $49.99 USD each year, selected by the member. No free trial is included. The selected plan renews automatically until canceled. Any applicable tax and exact total must be disclosed before the member agrees and authorizes payment. Access starts after a successful verified payment; no music license or copyright interest is included in membership.

Cancellation and refunds

Members will be able to cancel future renewal in their account or through PayPal automatic payments. Cancel before the next scheduled billing date to avoid renewal. Verified paid access continues through the paid period, subject to suspension for fraud, abuse, refund or reversal. Cancellation does not by itself refund a past charge. Contact support for billing errors, duplicate charges or unavailable paid services. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Community conduct

Use the community respectfully. Do not post threats, harassment, spam, fraud, private information without consent, or material you lack permission to share. Members retain rights in their own content and permit the service to display it to the intended audience for community operation. Report harmful content. Account restrictions may be necessary to protect members and the service.

Privacy and accounts

The planned service stores account identifiers, chosen profile information, membership status, transaction references, messages and reports. PayPal processes payment credentials. Cloudflare provides hosting and database services. Directory visibility is opt-in and available to verified members. Members may list music or other professional services, but BruA1 does not verify credentials, guarantee availability or act as the contracting party for work arranged between members. Do not post private contact information or exact residential addresses without consent. Do not share login credentials or sensitive information in posts. Final privacy terms, retention, account recovery and data request procedures will be completed before enrollment opens.

Support

Proposed support contact: brua1@brua1.com. No paid service is currently open.
